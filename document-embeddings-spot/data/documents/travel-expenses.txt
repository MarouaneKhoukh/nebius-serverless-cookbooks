Travel expenses and reimbursement

For approved business travel, keep itemized receipts for trains, flights, hotels and meals. Submit the receipts with the trip purpose and project code in the expense portal. The sample policy asks travelers to submit within fourteen days of returning.

Ask your manager to approve the report before finance reimburses it. Personal purchases and missing receipts need a separate explanation. Check the travel budget before booking a non-refundable ticket. This fictional policy exists only as a document search example.
